Prove an Answer Is Best

On this page

Start here

Which rectangle with perimeter 20 has the largest area? Finding a rectangle with area 25 gives you a candidate. To prove it is best, you also need a reason every allowed rectangle has area at most 25.

Worked example

Let the positive side lengths be aa and bb. The perimeter condition gives a+b=10a+b=10. Then

4ab=(a+b)2−(a−b)2≤100.4ab=(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2\le100.

Every area is at most 25. The 5×55\times5 rectangle attains 25, so the upper bound and the example meet. The identity and the example are a certificate, a short way for someone else to verify the optimum.

One way to get a bound is a relaxation. Allow more choices than the original rules permit and solve that easier problem. For a maximum, this can only raise the best value, so it gives an upper bound. Check whether an original choice actually reaches it.

Try it

  • Practice. With perimeter 22 and positive integer sides, find the maximum area and prove it. What does allowing fractional sides change?
  • Stretch. You have positive integer sides summing to n≥2n\ge2. Show why moving 1 unit from the longer side to the shorter side improves the area whenever the sides differ by at least 2.

Check your work

Practice. The same identity gives ab≤121/4=30.25ab\le121/4=30.25. Integer areas are therefore at most 30, and sides 5 and 6 attain 30. Fractional sides can reach 30.25 with sides 5.5 and 5.5.

Stretch. If b≥a+2b\ge a+2, then (a+1)(b−1)−ab=b−a−1>0(a+1)(b-1)-ab=b-a-1>0. Repeat until the sides differ by at most 1. This is an exchange argument because it rules out any unbalanced optimum by improving it.
