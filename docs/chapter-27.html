Check Units and Scaling

On this page

  • Start here

  • Try it

Start here

At 60 kilometers per hour, a 90-kilometer trip takes 90/60=1.590/60=1.5 hours. The units tell you to divide distance by speed. Multiplying would give square kilometers per hour, which is a different kind of quantity.

Worked example

Double every edge of a solid cube. Each face becomes four times as large, while the volume becomes eight times as large. In general, multiplying every length by kk multiplies area by k2k^2 and volume by k3k^3. This is the square–cube law.

Suppose the cube represents a block of uniform material. Its mass grows with volume, and the area available for a coating grows with surface area. A scaled block therefore needs eight times the material and four times the coating when k=2k=2, assuming the same density and coating use per unit area. Ratios can change when the shape remains identical.

Try it

  • Practice. A cube's edge length triples. By what factors do surface area, volume, and the ratio of area to volume change?
  • Stretch. A vehicle travels at speed vv for time tt. If the distance is modeled as CvatbCv^a t^b, where CC is a pure number, use units to find aa and bb. What does this say about CC?

Check your work

Practice. The area factor is 9, the volume factor is 27, and the ratio changes by a factor of 1/31/3.

Stretch. The length unit appears with power aa, so a=1a=1. The time unit appears with power b−ab-a, so b=1b=1. Units give the form CvtCvt. If vv is the constant speed over the full time, the definition of speed supplies C=1C=1.
