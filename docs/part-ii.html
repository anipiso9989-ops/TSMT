Part II checkpoint

Choose any 11 distinct integers from 1 through 20. Prove that some two sum to 21. Then give ten choices with no such pair. Try to derive the boxes from the desired sum before recalling the method's name. In your review, explain which half proves the guarantee and which shows the number of choices is sharp.

Further challenge

Start with a row of nn tails. A move flips two adjacent coins. Characterize exactly which final patterns are reachable. A parity argument gives a necessary condition. For the other direction, pair off the desired heads and ask what happens when you flip every adjacent pair along the interval between two paired positions.
