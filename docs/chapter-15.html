Change the Representation

Start here

A problem about who can reach whom by road may be easier on a sketch. A problem about area may be easier in algebra. A representation is how you choose to describe the objects and relationships.

Worked example

Find real numbers x,yx,y with x+y=10x+y=10 and xy=21xy=21. The two numbers sit equally far from their average, 5. Write x=5+tx=5+t and y=5−ty=5-t. The product becomes 25−t225-t^2. Setting it to 21 gives t2=4t^2=4, so t=2t=2 or t=−2t=-2. The ordered pairs are (7,3)(7,3) and (3,7)(3,7).

The new description builds in the sum condition. It covers all solutions because t=x−5t=x-5 and then y=5−ty=5-t. Check the original conditions after translating back. Some transformations, such as squaring both sides, can admit extra candidates.

Try it

  • Practice. Find two numbers with sum 12 and product 32 using the same representation.
  • Stretch. Solve x+5=x−1\sqrt{x+5}=x-1. State the restriction before squaring and check the candidates in the original equation.
